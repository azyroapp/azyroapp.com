Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

At AzyroApp, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy describes how the Bible app handles your information. We designed Bible with privacy at its core — your personal data stays on your device, and we have no interest in collecting, selling, or sharing it.

📖 About the Bible App

Bible is a mobile application developed by AzyroApp that allows you to read, study, and share the Word of God. The app includes multiple Bible versions in various languages, reading tools such as favorites, pins, reading progress tracking, and advanced verse search capabilities.

Bible is designed to function as a standalone tool on your device. It does not require an account, does not connect to external servers for its core functionality, and does not collect personal information.

🔒 Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Bible is built with a privacy-first architecture. This means:

  • All your data — favorites, pins, reading progress, preferences, and settings — is stored locally on your device
  • The app does not require user registration or an account to function
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or any form of behavioral monitoring
  • We do not display advertisements of any kind
  • We do not create user profiles or track your reading habits

In simple terms: what you do in the Bible app stays on your device. We cannot see it, we do not want to see it, and we have built the app so that we never will.

💾 Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your iPhone:

Reading Data

  • Favorite verses you have bookmarked
  • Pins you have added to verses
  • Chapters marked as read and reading progress statistics
  • Your selected Bible version preference

App Preferences

  • Font size and reading display settings
  • Color theme preferences
  • Notification settings and schedules
  • Language preference

Purchase Information

  • Your subscription or purchase status (active/inactive) is stored in your device's encrypted Keychain
  • This is used solely to determine whether to unlock premium content
  • We do not store payment details, credit card numbers, or billing information

All of this data is stored using Apple's standard secure storage mechanisms (UserDefaults, Keychain, and SQLite databases) and is protected by your device's built-in encryption.

☁️ iCloud Synchronization

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, the Bible app uses Apple's NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore to synchronize certain data across your Apple devices:

What syncs via iCloud:

  • Your favorite verses
  • Your pinned verses
  • Your reading progress per Bible version
  • Your reading statistics

Important details about iCloud sync:

  • This synchronization is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud infrastructure
  • AzyroApp does not operate any servers that receive, process, or store this data
  • Only you can access your iCloud data through your Apple ID
  • You can disable iCloud sync at any time through your device's Settings
  • If you disable iCloud, the app continues to function normally using only local storage

For more information about how Apple handles iCloud data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

🔔 Notifications

Bible may send you local notifications if you have granted permission:

  • Daily reading reminders at your preferred time
  • These notifications are generated entirely on your device by iOS
  • No notification data is sent to any external server
  • You can manage or disable notifications at any time through iOS Settings → Notifications → Bible

The app will request notification permission during the initial setup (onboarding). You may accept or decline this request. Your choice does not affect the app's core functionality.

💳 Purchases, Subscriptions, and Payments

Bible offers premium content through in-app purchases managed entirely by Apple's App Store:

Payment Processing

  • All transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store
  • AzyroApp never receives, processes, or stores your payment information
  • We do not have access to your credit card number, billing address, or any financial data
  • Apple handles all payment security, fraud prevention, and transaction processing

What We Store Locally

  • A boolean flag indicating whether you have an active premium subscription or purchase
  • This flag is stored in your device's encrypted Keychain
  • It is used solely to determine whether to display premium content

Purchase Verification

  • The app verifies your purchase status using Apple's StoreKit 2 framework
  • Verification happens directly between your device and Apple's servers
  • AzyroApp does not operate any server-side receipt validation

Subscription Management

  • You can manage, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel subscriptions through iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions
  • Refund requests are handled exclusively by Apple according to their refund policies

For more information about how Apple handles purchases, please refer to Apple's subscription management guide.

🚫 Information We Do NOT Collect

To be completely transparent, here is a comprehensive list of what we do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Device identifiers: We do not collect your device ID, IDFA, or any unique hardware identifiers
  • Location data: We do not access or collect your geographic location
  • Usage analytics: We do not track which verses you read, how long you spend reading, or your navigation patterns
  • Crash reports: We do not collect crash logs or diagnostic data
  • Network information: We do not collect your IP address, network provider, or connection type
  • Contacts or calendar: We do not access your contacts, calendar, or any other personal data on your device
  • Photos or media: We do not access your photo library, camera, or microphone (except for text-to-speech, which is processed entirely on-device by iOS)

🛡️ Data Security

Although we do not collect your data, we take the security of locally stored data seriously:

  • Sensitive data (such as purchase status) is stored in Apple's Keychain, which uses hardware-level encryption
  • All other data is stored using Apple's standard secure storage mechanisms
  • The app does not transmit data over the network (except for iCloud sync handled by Apple and purchase verification handled by Apple's StoreKit)
  • We follow Apple's security best practices for iOS app development

👶 Children's Privacy

Bible is rated 4+ on the App Store, meaning it is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, there is no risk of collecting data from children.

The app does not contain user-generated content, social features, or any mechanism that could expose children to inappropriate content or interactions.

Parents and guardians can feel confident that the Bible app is safe for use by children of all ages.

🗑️ Data Deletion and Control

You have complete control over all data associated with the Bible app:

Delete Local Data

  • Uninstall the app from your device — all local data (favorites, pins, progress, settings) is permanently deleted
  • You can also reset specific data within the app's settings

Delete iCloud Data

  • Go to iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Bible
  • Delete the app's iCloud data to remove synced favorites, pins, and progress

Cancel Subscriptions

  • Go to iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Bible
  • Cancel or modify your subscription at any time

Since we do not store any data on our servers, there is no need to submit a data deletion request to us. All your data is under your direct control.

🌍 International Users

Bible is available worldwide. Because we do not collect, process, or transfer personal data, there are no cross-border data transfer concerns. All data remains on your device and, if enabled, in your personal iCloud account managed by Apple.

Regardless of where you are located, the same privacy protections apply to all users equally.

📱 Third-Party Services

The Bible app integrates with the following Apple services only:

  • Apple StoreKit 2: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions. Managed entirely by Apple.
  • Apple iCloud (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore): For optional data synchronization across your devices. Managed entirely by Apple.
  • Apple AVFoundation: For text-to-speech functionality. All processing happens on your device.

We do not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, social media platforms, or data brokers.

  • We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • For significant changes, we may provide notice through the app
  • The updated policy will be effective immediately upon posting

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
